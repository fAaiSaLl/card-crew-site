Euchre Scoring Rules: Points for Makers, Marches, Loners and Euchres

Updated: 2026-11-12

Short answer: the team that called trump (the makers) scores 1 point for taking 3 or 4 of the 5 tricks and 2 points for taking all 5. A player who goes alone and takes all 5 scores 4 points. If the makers take fewer than 3 tricks, they are euchred and the other team scores 2 points. First team to 10 wins.

The scoring table

What happenedPointsWho scores
Makers take 3 or 4 tricks1Makers
Makers take all 5 tricks (a march)2Makers
Lone maker takes 3 or 4 tricks1Makers
Lone maker takes all 5 tricks4Makers
Makers take 0, 1 or 2 tricks (euchred)2Defenders

Who are the makers?

Whoever orders up the turned card or names trump in the second round makes trump for their team. Only the makers have to reach 3 tricks; the defenders just have to stop them.

Going alone

A maker who is confident can play without their partner. It's only worth it if you expect all 5 tricks: a loner who takes 3 or 4 still scores just 1 point.

Worked example

Score is 7 to 6. Your partner orders up hearts and goes alone, takes all 5 tricks: +4, you win 11 to 6. If instead they had taken only 2 tricks, the other team would score 2 and lead 8 to 7.

Common questions

Do defenders get more for euchring a loner?

In standard scoring, no: a euchre is 2 points either way. Some tables give the defenders 4 when a defender also goes alone; agree on it before the game.

Can a game go past 10?

The game ends as soon as a team reaches 10 or more, so a final score of 11 or 12 is normal.
